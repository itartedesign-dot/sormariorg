Who we are

Oenologists, with muddy hands and shoes

Or better, we are a team of oenologists with muddy hands and shoes. That's how close to the vines we work. That's why we also like being called “winemakers”. With Mario Sorbello in the lead, we offer 360° oenological consulting services. From nothing to finding the right field, to turning it into your vineyard, your winery, wine, bottle, etiquette.
