Winemaking philosophy

Our principles

We believe in doing as little as possible, and only when it truly matters. Throughout the whole journey from grape to bottle, we stay close — watching, checking, tasting. Our role is to be ready: present with scientific know-how, stepping in only when nature calls for it.

Think of the winery as a delivery room, and the wine as a newborn finding its own way into the world.

This respect for nature's own process is what shapes the wines we make: organic, no-intervention, and biodynamic.

Discover

Our aim

Our goal is simple to state, demanding to achieve: the best wine a place is capable of giving. We work to reveal the terroir — its hidden character, its quiet strengths — so the result reflects both the land and the ambitions of the people behind it.

No two places are the same, and we treat none of them as if they were. That is why every wine we help create is one of a kind.

As singular as where it comes from. As singular as you are.

Our winemaking process

Step 1 — Nature does the first work, ripening the grape in its own time.

Step 2 — We harvest by hand, carrying each cluster to the winery with the same care it grew with.

Step 3 — From there, science takes its turn: present, attentive, guiding the grape as it becomes wine.

A wine is a living thing — born in the vineyard, raised in the cellar. Fittingly, the French call this stage “élevage”, the Spanish “crianza”: both words that simply mean, to raise.

Test Wine

Laboratory Tests Wine

Harvest means full speed in the laboratory too.

During the grape harvest (September-November), samples arrive at our laboratory every day from different parts of Greece and Cyprus.

Grapes approaching harvest: musts undergoing fermentation, wines nearing the end of fermentation. Each sample captures a different moment in the same journey, giving us the information we need to monitor its progress and make the right decisions at every stage.

These days, the rhythm in our laboratory is constant: samples, analyses, results, evaluation… and then we start all over again.

At the same time, the rest of our team is out in vineyards and wineries across Greece and Cyprus. One side of our work simply cannot function without the other. Behind every decision we make at the vineyards or the wineries, lies also in the work of our laboratory team: the analyses, results and data that allow us to understand what is happening and determine how we need to move forward.

It is a constant collaboration between the laboratory and the team working outside: different roles, but all essential to keeping the whole operation moving.

The samples reach the laboratory. The data goes back to the field. And the harvest continues..!
